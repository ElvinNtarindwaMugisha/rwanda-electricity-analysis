Key Findings

What the data tells us

Fastest expansion period: 2014–2017 — EDPRS II and the establishment of Rwanda Energy Group (REG/EDCL) drove three consecutive years of 5–8 percentage point annual gains. National access nearly quadrupled from 11.5% (2013) to 43% (2018).
Off-grid solar is a major and growing channel — 25% of Rwanda's 84.6% total connectivity comes from Solar Home Systems (SHS) and mini-grids. This represents ~850,000 households and is the segment with the greatest growth potential for renewable energy businesses.
2023 recorded the largest single-year jump: +13.3pp — from 50.6% to 63.9%. The 2030 universal access target requires closing the remaining 15.4% gap, concentrated in remote rural areas where off-grid solutions are most cost-effective.

Policy Milestones

How government strategy drove acceleration

  • 2008 Government launches EDPRS I with first household electrification targets (70,000 → 200,000 connections). Energy Sector-Wide Approach (eSWAp) established with World Bank support.Policy
  • 2009 Baseline: only 6% of households have electricity. Rwanda commits to rapid expansion under Vision 2020.Baseline
  • 2013 EDPRS II launched. Rwanda Energy Group (REG) and EDCL established in 2014. Annual performance contracts (imihigo) introduced for accountability and targets.Reform
  • 2018 Ministerial guidelines for mini-grids issued. Solar Home Systems (SHS) mandated via Development Bank of Rwanda. Urban access reaches ~75% while off-grid rural push accelerates.Off-grid push
  • 2020 National access reaches ~46.6%. Rwanda ranks 11th globally and 3rd in Africa for electrification speed 2010–2020. COVID-19 causes minor dip to 45.2% — the only recorded decline.Milestone
  • 2024 75% household electricity access achieved — from 6% in just 15 years. 100% of health centres and all sector-level administrative facilities connected. 84% of schools electrified.Achievement
  • 2025 Total connectivity reaches 84.6% (59.6% on-grid + 25% off-grid solar). Rwanda targets 100% electricity access by 2030.Current
